01 · Workspace

It starts at the desk

Every workstation, TV and phone gets its own dedicated, tested connection. No daisy-chained switches under desks.

02 · Terminations

Plates on the wall, cable in the wall

Keystone jacks snap into labeled wall plates, and each run goes up inside the wall. Nothing lying across the floor.

03 · Pathways

Above the ceiling, done properly

Cables bundle into a trunk on J-hooks. Access points blanket every room, so phones roam AP to AP without dropping. Ceiling speakers carry music and paging, and cameras cover every entrance.

04 · MDF · Main Distribution Frame

Welcome to the Mainframe

Where the core technology lives. The main trunk arrives on a ladder rack and drops into the rack inside a protective sleeve, the way code requires.

IDF · Intermediate Distribution Frame

Welcome to the Intermediate Frame

A smaller wall-mount rack closer to the users, with its own switches, patch panels and battery backup, tied back to the Mainframe (MDF) over fiber.

05 · Rack build

Built from the top down

The monitor shelf and fiber go in first, then patch panels and switches, the door access hub, the firewall, modem, PDU, audio amp, camera recorder and, at the bottom, battery backup.

06 · Patching

Clean patch cords. Every port.

Each switch sits right above its patch panel, so every cord is one short, rounded loop, color-coded by what it powers. Lighted patch cords glow at the switch so every port is easy to trace.

07 · Backbone

Fiber core, live WAN

Fiber runs from the gateway to every switch and out to each IDF, and the modem feeds the WAN. The lights come on.

08 · Security

Every camera, one screen

Entry and exit doors, parking, hallways and the network room, recorded around the clock and live on the rack monitor.
